Flexibility is the key

The right engagement model is the first step to customer success — we offer models that flex with your needs.

Models for Choice

Pick what works for your project

Our engagement models are focused on flexibility. Customer requirements change — our models change with them.

Fixed Pricing

Recommended when: requirements are clear, scope changes are minimal, you already have experience with our team.

Advantages: pre-estimated cost, maximum saving potential, delivery on time and on budget, outcome guaranteed by contract.

Time & Material

Recommended when: requirements are evolving, you need maximum flexibility, or the project scope is not yet fully defined.

Advantages: full transparency on effort, ability to reprioritise features at any sprint, pay only for what is delivered.

Dedicated Team

Recommended when: you want to expand your in-house team with offshore specialists or need a fully managed remote development team.

Advantages: deep integration with your processes, consistent team knowledge, rapid scaling up or down.

Not sure which model fits your project?

Our consultants will recommend the right engagement approach for your situation.

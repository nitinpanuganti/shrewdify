QA & Testing

AI-powered quality assurance that auto-generates test cases, catches visual regressions across every browser, and prioritises the tests most likely to find your next bug — running 60% faster as a result.

Overview

What we deliver

Quality assurance is the last line of defence before production, but manual testing can't keep pace with modern development velocity. We apply AI across three dimensions of testing to close that gap: LLMs analyse your specifications and codebase to auto-generate test suites that cover edge cases human testers consistently miss; computer vision detects pixel-level UI regressions across browsers and devices in every build; and ML-driven test prioritisation ranks which tests are most likely to catch the current code change, cutting suite runtime by 60% without sacrificing coverage. The result is quality that accelerates release cycles rather than bottlenecking them.

Our Process

How we work

  1. Assess

    We audit your current test coverage, identify critical untested paths, and map the risk areas where AI-generated tests will deliver the highest value.

  2. Generate

    We run AI test generation against your codebase and specifications, review the output with your team, and integrate it into the CI pipeline.

  3. Optimise

    We instrument test prioritisation and visual regression into every build, measuring suite runtime and coverage improvements sprint over sprint.

  4. Embed

    We train your engineering team in shift-left quality practices, ensuring AI-powered testing becomes a permanent competitive advantage.

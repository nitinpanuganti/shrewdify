Mobile App Development

Native and cross-platform apps that go beyond beautiful design — with on-device AI that personalises every interaction and keeps users coming back.

Overview

What we deliver

We design and build iOS and Android applications that combine polished user experience with the intelligent behaviour users now expect as standard. By embedding TensorFlow Lite and Core ML models directly on-device, our apps deliver AI-powered features even without a network connection. Our personalisation engine continuously analyses in-app behaviour to adapt content and offers to each individual, while smart notification models ensure your messages reach users at exactly the right moment — dramatically lifting engagement and cutting churn across the full user lifecycle.

AI-Powered

The AI advantage

AI is no longer an optional add-on for mobile — it is the core of what makes an app sticky. We bake intelligence into the data layer, the UX, and the communication layer of every app we ship.

On-Device ML Models

We embed TensorFlow Lite and Core ML models directly in the app binary, enabling offline-capable intelligent features with no server round-trip latency.

AI-Powered Personalisation

Behavioural analysis dynamically personalises content, layout, and offers for each user — turning a generic app into a tailored experience that drives loyalty.

Smart Push Notifications

ML predicts the optimal time and message to re-engage each individual user, replacing broadcast blasts with precision outreach that users actually welcome.

Our Process

How we work

  1. Discover

    We analyse your target users, map the user journey, and identify where AI features will deliver the greatest retention impact.

  2. Design

    UX designers and ML engineers collaborate on prototypes that validate AI-driven interactions before a line of code is written.

  3. Deliver

    Two-week sprints produce tested, store-ready increments with on-device models trained in parallel and battery-profiled continuously.

  4. Optimise

    Post-launch, we monitor engagement metrics and retrain personalisation models monthly to keep the app improving over time.

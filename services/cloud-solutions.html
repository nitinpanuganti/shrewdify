Cloud Solutions

Cloud infrastructure designed for resilience and efficiency — with AIOps that continuously right-sizes spend, predicts demand, and auto-remediates security drift before it becomes a risk.

Overview

What we deliver

We design, migrate, and operate cloud platforms on AWS, Azure, and GCP that are architected for the long term — not just lift-and-shifted for the short term. AI is embedded at every operational layer: ML continuously analyses your spend patterns, identifies idle resources, and enforces right-sizing to cut cloud bills by up to 35% without touching performance. Predictive scaling reads leading indicators of demand and provisions capacity before spikes hit, while our autonomous security posture management scans configurations continuously, detects drift from policy baselines, and auto-remediates low-risk misconfigurations before they become incidents.

Our Process

How we work

  1. Assess

    We audit your current infrastructure, spend, and security posture — producing a migration or optimisation roadmap with projected savings and risk reduction.

  2. Design

    We design a target architecture for reliability, cost efficiency, and security — with AIOps tooling selected and integrated from the outset.

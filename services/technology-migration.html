Technology Migration

Platform and cloud migrations de-risked by ML — scoring every component for risk before go-live, triggering automated rollback in under two minutes if needed, and validating 100% output parity in parallel.

Overview

What we deliver

Technology migrations fail most often for two reasons: risks that weren't identified before go-live, and no reliable way to confirm the new system behaves identically to the old one. We eliminate both. Our ML risk scoring engine assesses every component, dependency, and data set in advance, producing a prioritised safety map that covers 90% of risk before a single production byte moves. Once live, anomaly detection monitors the system continuously and triggers automatic rollback in under two minutes if error rates spike. And parallel validation testing runs live traffic through both old and new systems simultaneously, diffing outputs to guarantee complete parity — not just assumed parity.

Our Process

How we work

  1. Assess

    ML risk scoring analyses every component, data set, and integration — producing a prioritised risk register and a sequenced migration plan before any work begins.

  2. Prepare

    We instrument rollback triggers and parallel validation harnesses in staging, validating their operation under simulated load before touching production.

  3. Migrate

    We execute the migration in phases with parallel validation running throughout — only decommissioning source systems once parity is mathematically confirmed.

  4. Stabilise

    Post-migration monitoring continues for an agreed period, with rollback triggers active and performance baselines tracked to confirm sustained equivalence.

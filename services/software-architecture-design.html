Software Architecture Design

Principled, AI-informed architecture that makes the right call faster — with automated performance profiling and a full technical debt map before a single sprint begins.

Overview

What we deliver

Architecture decisions made without complete information create compounding problems for years. We combine the experience of senior architects with AI tooling that eliminates the information gaps that cause bad architectural calls. Our AI Architecture Advisor analyses your requirements and proposes ranked patterns with explicit trade-off explanations, so your team debates options rather than guessing at them. Automated performance profiling models the impact of architectural choices before you commit, and a technical debt scanner produces a quantified, business-impact-weighted map of your existing codebase so you know exactly what you're building on top of.

AI-Powered

The AI advantage

AI gives architects a data-driven lens on every decision — from pattern selection to performance modelling to debt quantification — so choices are made faster and with greater confidence.

AI Architecture Advisor

Analyses your requirements and proposes ranked architecture patterns with explicit trade-off explanations — turning weeks of whiteboard debate into structured, data-backed decisions.

Automated Performance Profiling

ML identifies bottlenecks and models the performance impact of proposed architectural changes before you write code — eliminating costly late-stage surprises.

Technical Debt Quantification

AI scans the codebase and produces a prioritised debt map with business impact estimates, so teams can address debt strategically rather than reactively.

Our Process

How we work

  1. Discover

    We run a technical debt scan and requirements analysis in parallel — giving us a complete picture of what you need to build and what you're building on top of.

  2. Advise

    Our AI Architecture Advisor produces ranked patterns and trade-off analyses, which our architects present with specific recommendations and rationale.

  3. Model

    We run automated performance profiles on the proposed architecture, validating it against your SLOs before the first sprint is planned.

  4. Document

    We produce Architecture Decision Records for every major choice, giving your team a living audit trail that supports future decisions and onboarding.

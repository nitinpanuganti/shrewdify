Suggestive Market Analysis

AI-powered market intelligence that monitors thousands of signals daily, reveals how your market perceives competitors, and ranks every growth opportunity by fit and potential.

Overview

What we deliver

Strategic decisions made on stale market data carry compounding risk. We build AI-powered market intelligence systems that monitor over 10,000 news sources, regulatory filings, social channels, and patent databases daily — surfacing competitive signals the moment they emerge rather than weeks later. Multi-source sentiment analysis reveals exactly how your target market perceives competitors, products, and emerging trends across languages and channels. And our opportunity scoring model ranks market segments by growth potential, competition density, and fit with your specific capabilities, giving your strategy and product teams a clear, data-backed view of where to place their next bet.

Our Process

How we work

  1. Scope

    We define the competitive landscape, signal sources, and strategic questions that the intelligence system should answer — aligning outputs to your board-level decisions.

  2. Build

    We configure data ingestion pipelines across news, social, regulatory, and patent sources — training NLP models on your domain vocabulary for maximum signal precision.

  3. Analyse

    We produce an initial competitive landscape report, sentiment baseline, and opportunity scoring model — presented with strategic recommendations.

  4. Monitor

    Daily briefings, weekly trend digests, and on-demand deep dives keep your team ahead of the market as the competitive landscape evolves.

IT Strategy Consulting

Actionable technology roadmaps grounded in AI readiness assessment, continuous market intelligence, and rigorous build-vs-buy analysis — delivered in six weeks.

Overview

What we deliver

Technology strategy fails when it is decoupled from commercial reality. We bring together senior architects, data scientists, and commercial strategists to produce roadmaps that are specific, sequenced, and grounded in numbers. Our AI readiness assessment benchmarks your data assets, team capabilities, and infrastructure against a proven maturity framework, producing a prioritised list of AI initiatives with realistic timelines and ROI projections. Continuous technology trend intelligence — drawn from research papers, patent filings, and market signals — ensures your multi-year roadmap adapts to a fast-moving landscape rather than locking you into last year's assumptions.

Our Process

How we work

  1. Assess

    We run an AI readiness assessment covering data maturity, team capability, infrastructure, and competitive positioning over two weeks.

  2. Analyse

    We model build-vs-buy scenarios, evaluate vendors, and benchmark your architecture against industry patterns to identify the highest-value opportunities.

  3. Roadmap

    We deliver a sequenced, costed technology roadmap with OKRs, success metrics, and an ROI projection for every initiative.

  4. Enable

    We support change management, vendor negotiations, and the first sprint of execution to ensure the strategy lands rather than sits on a shelf.

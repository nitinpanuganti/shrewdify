Progrow

A mobile-first recruitment and interview management platform — connecting job seekers with interviewers through AI-powered matching, structured boards, and real-time collaboration.

Product Overview

Hiring and growing, reimagined for mobile

Progrow eliminates the friction from recruitment by putting candidates and interviewers on the same platform. Job seekers build a rich professional profile, browse an open interview board, and track every stage of their career journey — all from their phone.

Interviewers and companies post opportunities on the board, review matched candidates using AI scoring, conduct structured interviews, and manage the full pipeline from a clean mobile interface. Real-time chat keeps communication instant and contextual throughout the process.

AI-Powered

Smarter hiring through AI

Progrow uses machine learning to reduce time-to-hire, surface the best-fit candidates, and give both sides of the interview table actionable insights.

AI Candidate Matching

ML models score candidate profiles against open opportunities based on skills, experience, and history — surfacing the strongest fits before human review begins.

Career Progression Analytics

AI analyses interview history and profile trajectories to surface growth patterns, skill gaps, and recommended next steps for each candidate.

Smart Notifications

ML predicts optimal notification timing and content — re-engaging candidates and interviewers when they're most likely to take action, not just at fixed intervals.

Five Core Screens

The full recruitment journey in five tabs

Every tab is purpose-built for a different stage of the career and hiring journey.

Profile

A rich professional profile with dedicated sections for Skills, Experience, Education, Achievements, Projects, Publications, Objectives, and Career Rankings. Candidates build a verified professional identity that travels with them across every application.

Interview History

Chronological record of every interview — applied, accepted, completed, and declined. View feedback, scores, and progression data over time. AI analyses the history to surface growth patterns and skill gap recommendations.

Interview Board

An open, live board of interview opportunities posted by companies and interviewers. Candidates browse, filter by skills and role, and apply in one tap. AI scoring surfaces the best-fit matches for each candidate's profile before they apply.

Accept Interviews (Interviewer View)

Interviewers and companies manage their pipeline — review AI-ranked candidate shortlists, accept or defer applications, schedule interview slots, rate candidates post-interview, and track the full pipeline from a structured, mobile-first interface.

Settings

Profile visibility controls, notification preferences, account management, and privacy settings. Candidates control who sees their profile. Interviewers manage company branding, notification cadence, and pipeline defaults.

Real-Time Chat

Socket.IO-powered in-app messaging between candidates and interviewers. Presence indicators, typing notifications, and message history. No email chains — all communication happens in context, linked to the active interview.

  • Settings, notification preferences & account management
